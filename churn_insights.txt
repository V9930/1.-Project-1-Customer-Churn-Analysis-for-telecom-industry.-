Customer Churn Analysis Insights

1. Churn Distribution:
Most customer did not churn, but a significant portion left the telecom service.

2. Churn by Contract Type:
Customers with month-to-month contracts have the highest churn rate compared to one-year or two-year contracts.

3. Monthly Charges vs Churn:
Customers paying higher monthly charges tend to churn more frequently.

4. Tenure Distribution:
Customers with shorter tenure are more likely to churn.

5. Churn by Internet Service:
Certain internet sevice types show higher churn compared to other.
